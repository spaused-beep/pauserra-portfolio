About me

Strategic UX Leader and Design System Expert with 20+ years of international experience across institutions like the European Patent Office, WIPO, the European Commission, leading banks, insurers, and healthcare providers, as well as NGOs like Amnesty International, Oxfam, and the Red Cross.

My work lives at the intersection of creativity, strategy, and human connection — designing and leading digital products and ecosystems that scale while keeping people at the center. From building the EPO Design System to revamping Mahou-San Miguel's Nexho® eCommerce platform, I bring strategic vision, research, usability expertise, and visual craft to every engagement.

Colleagues describe me as calm, generous, and empathetic — someone who builds trust, connects cultures, and motivates teams to do their best work. Beyond work, I draw inspiration from art, music, travel, and cultural exploration — passions that fuel curiosity and bring fresh perspectives into the systems I design.

My mission: to connect people, products, and strategy through design that is inclusive, meaningful, and built to last.
